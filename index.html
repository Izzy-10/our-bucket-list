Getting started

This is an example bucket list. Edit it into your own plan: change the tickets, add yours, and delete what doesn't fit.

  1. Add your names in the two boxes below.
  2. Tap Edit or Delete under a ticket, or use Add your own ticket at the bottom of the list.
  3. Tap a ticket when you've done it together to stamp it.
  4. Live now helps you do one: a timer, simple steps and a plan date.
  5. Memories keeps a date, a note, a song and a photo for each ticket you finish.
  6. Story turns your memories into one picture you can save and share.
  7. Tip: in your browser menu, choose Add to Home screen to open it like an app.
  8. Sync your phones (under your names) lets both of you see the same list.

    Your list, photos and notes are saved on your own device. If you turn on sync, they are also stored in a private room in a Firebase database, and anyone with your secret code can see and edit them. Don't share the code with people you don't trust. We don't sell or share your data.
